WHY THE GATE STILL FAILED
The generic technical test used the arithmetic mean of the complete one-shot
as a DC-offset test. That is invalid for asymmetric/clipped transient events
and rejected almost every non-RAVE preset even though sonic parity passed.

STAGE 6.5
- Whole-event mean is reported but no longer used as a DC failure criterion.
- Persistent DC/runaway tail is tested after decay at 3.5-4.0 seconds:
  tail RMS < -50 dBFS and abs(tail mean) < 1e-4.
- Adds 650-750 ms envelope parity to the user-approved A anchors.
- Applies residual safety to all 50 factory presets and 25 randomizer trials.
- DSP, preset values and parameter IDs are unchanged.
