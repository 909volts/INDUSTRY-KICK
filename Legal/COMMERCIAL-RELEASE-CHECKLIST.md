# 909VOLTS commercial release checklist

Complete these items before distributing a paid public build:

- insert the registered legal entity or sole-trader name, business address, support email and website in the EULA;
- choose governing law, courts and any required alternative-dispute-resolution wording;
- have a qualified lawyer review the EULA for each main sales territory;
- publish a privacy notice before adding accounts, licence activation, analytics or telemetry;
- define refund and cancellation terms for downloaded digital content without attempting to exclude mandatory consumer rights;
- implement affirmative acceptance of the EULA during checkout or installation and retain the accepted version number;
- verify ownership and commercial licences for the 909VOLTS name, logo, fonts, artwork, source code and every bundled IR;
- run trade-mark, company-name, domain and social-handle clearance for 909VOLTS and INDUSTRY KICK;
- decide whether licences are single-user, studio, educational or subscription-based and publish the corresponding prices;
- code-sign the Windows installer and executable before public sale;
- archive release binaries, source, hashes, licence text and customer-facing product descriptions for every version;
- test the final installer, uninstall flow and VST3 scan on a clean supported Windows machine.
